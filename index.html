FAQ

Questions students ask us

Do I need a business idea before I apply?

No. Module 1 helps you find and validate an idea. Many students arrive with only a problem they care about.

Can I follow Peachology while studying full-time?

Yes. The program is designed around academic schedules, with evening and weekend sessions and about four hours of work per week.

Who can apply?

University and high-school students, recent graduates and young entrepreneurs. Schools and youth organizations can also enroll groups.

Will I really launch a business?

Yes. Every participant builds a real offer and works toward their first paying customers before the program ends.

How do I join an information session?

Use the “Attend an info session” button below to reserve a seat at the next online or on-campus session.

Ready to learn, build and launch?

Applications for the next cohort are open. Apply in five minutes, or join a free information session to meet the team and past student founders.
